About S2M CENTER Help

Thank you for choosing the S2M CENTER. These help files were created in order to make your experience with this product more enjoyable and productive. We welcome your feedback so please let us know if you find this
information useful by emailing us at tech@cabinetvision.com.
The format of these Help files should closely follow the layout of the S2M CENTER itself. Also, you may press the F1 key on your keyboard to see help on the current window.
In order to get the most from using the S2M CENTER help files, it is important to first know some terms of communication that will be used throughout the Help System.
First, any time that you are to navigate through menus to perform a certain function, the menu sequence will appear as follows:
Main | Help | Help Topics
This tells you that you should click on the Main tab, then click on Help, and finally, click on Help Topics in order to get to the specified location.

Secondly, it is important to understand the terminology used in describing movements and clicks of the mouse.

On a mouse with the typical configuration, the left button is used as a Select tool. The right button is used as an alternate select tool. Some mice have a third button or a wheel.

With this typical configuration, if you see the word "click" used in these help files, that means to click one time with the left mouse button on the object specified. A "double-click" means to click two times in relatively rapid
succession on the item or object specified. A "right-click" means to click one time with the right mouse button on the object specified. Also, you will be asked to "drag" objects with the mouse at certain times. This means that you
will hold the mouse over the specified object, click and hold the left mouse button, then move the mouse while still holding the left button until you reach the area that you were instructed to move the object to. Some objects may be
"dropped" by simply letting go of the left mouse button. Other objects must be "dropped" by clicking again when you reach the destination. This will be something that you will simply have to practice in order to master.

What Edges can be Pre-Milled / Pre-Banded

Pre-Banded edges can only be the Left, Right, Top & Bottom of the part while it is still a rectangle. Even if the part is shaped and banded on all edges only the L, R, T & Bottom edges will show the part size extended as
pre-banded since the part would be banded prior to being placed on the P2P / Router to be shaped.

Pre-Milled edges can be any pre-banded edge as well as any other edge that could be fed into a banding machine. Edges that are excluded would be arcs or any edge that couldn't be fed into the machine due to a collision
with another edge of the part. The part will be resized with only valid pre-mill edges extended.

fed into a banding machine and therefore the part size was not extended for these edges.

In this example the pre-mill edge is valid for all 5 edges while the pre-band edge would NOT be valid for the angle.

parts. Even if the radius was not banded, there is danger of the end trimmer cutting into the radius. For these reasons an edge adjacent to a radius is excluded.
