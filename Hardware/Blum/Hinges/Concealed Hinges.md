# Blum Concealed Hinges

Blum's full concealed (35mm cup, "Euro") hinge program. **CLIP top BLUMOTION**, **CLIP top** and **CLIP** are
mostly for frameless cabinets, or face frame with adapter plates. **COMPACT** is Blum's face frame hinge line.

Source: `Concealed Hinges Catalog (2025).pdf` (in this folder), Blum, Inc. 2025, 104 pages. Everything below
comes from that catalog. The boring-distance grids are drawings that came out garbled in the text copy.
Blum's overlay formula (below) rebuilds them, and anything else unreadable points to a catalog page.

## Terms and the overlay formula

| Abbr. | Meaning |
|---|---|
| B | Boring distance: cup edge to door edge (usually 3–7mm) |
| FD | Fixed distance: how far the cup overlays the side panel on a 0mm plate |
| H | Mounting plate height |
| OL | Overlay |
| R | Reveal (inset) |
| CD | Cup depth |
| T | Door thickness |
| SB | Mounting plate setback |

**Overlay: OL = FD + B − H.** For example, a CLIP top 110° straight-arm has FD 11. On a 0mm plate with B = 5,
that gives an overlay of 16mm. On a 3mm plate it gives 13mm.

- **Cup:** Ø35mm with Ø8 dowel holes. The cup center is 9.5mm from the door edge plus B, and the dowel holes
  are 45mm apart. Cup depth is usually 11–13mm, and all 35mm and 8mm holes need to be at least 13mm deep.
- **Arm crank sets the overlay type:**
  - **straight** = full overlay
  - **half-cranked** = partial or twin overlay, two doors on one partition
  - **full-cranked** = inset
- **Inset:** move the plate back by **T + 1.5mm**.
- **Minimum reveal:** there's a minimum reveal table for each hinge, based on a square-edge door with a 1mm
  radius. Thicker doors and larger B need more reveal, and profiled edges need more still (trial fit). For
  example, on the 110° hinges a 19mm door needs 0.9–1.0mm and a 26mm door needs 3.0–4.3mm.
- **Hinges per door:** Blum's chart (catalog p. 2) plots door height (20"–100") against weight, and gives
  2, 3, 4 or 5 hinges. The weight bands appear to be under 15, 15–30, 30–45 and 45–60 lb. It's a graphic, so
  the exact height breakpoints for each count didn't survive the text copy. Read them off the chart.
  **The spacing between the top and bottom hinges must be greater than the door width.**
- **Don't mix hinge types on one door.** For example, CLIP top with CLIP top BLUMOTION, or COMPACT with
  COMPACT BLUMOTION or COMPACT CLIP. Their geometry differs.
- **Ratings:** all Blum hinges meet ANSI/BHMA A156.9 Grade 2 and are for indoor use only. CLIP hinges and
  soft-close COMPACT hinges are tested to 200,000 cycles, and plain COMPACT to 100,000.

### Part number patterns
- **71B** = CLIP top BLUMOTION (soft-close built in), **71T / 73T / 75T** = CLIP top (self-close),
  **70T…TL** = CLIP top free swing (for TIP-ON), **71M / 70M** = CLIP.
- The digit after the angle code is the crank: **…35xx straight, …36xx half, …37xx full**. For example,
  71B3550 is straight, 71B3650 half-cranked and 71B3750 full-cranked.
- The last digits are the mounting: **…50 screw-on, …80 press-in, …90 INSERTA** (tool-free expanding dowels),
  **…8E EXPANDO**.
- **COMPACT overlays are in sixteenths of an inch:** .06 = 3/8", .08 = 1/2", .10 = 5/8", .12 = 3/4",
  .16 = 1", .20 = 1-1/4", .21 = 1-5/16", .22 = 1-3/8", .24 = 1-1/2", .25 = 1-9/16". **E8** is an edge-mount
  1/2".
- Finish: NI = nickel, ONS = onyx black.

## Angled hinges (CLIP top BLUMOTION, frameless)

For cabinet front angles from **−45° to +45° in 5° steps**, using angled hinges plus wedges, plate setback,
adjustment and plate height. The angle and a crank code are stamped on the hinge. **I = corner merge,
II = partial overlay, III = maximum overlay.**

- **Positive:** +15° 79B9494, +20° 79B9595, +30° II 79B9596, +30° III 79B9496, +45° I 79B9698, +45° II
  79B3598, +45° III 79B9498.
- **Negative:** −15° 79B3493, −30° 79B3491, −45° 79B3490.
- Standard 95° thick door hinges (71B9590 / 9690 / 9790) are also used in some angled setups.
- **Wedges:** −5° 171A5500 (2mm), +5° 171A5010 (4mm), +5° 171A5040 (5mm) and 171A5070 (7mm).

Pp. 39–46 have a numbered chart that gives the hinge, plate and wedge for every angle and application
(maximum overlay, partial overlay, corner merge, inset, mitered). It's a dense grid, so use the PDF.

## COMPACT: Blum's face frame hinges

All COMPACT hinges are for doors **up to 3/4" (19mm)**. They screw to the face frame with **834TH truss head
screws** (#8 × 3/4"). The cup setback (and MINIPRESS setback) is 20.5mm (13/16"). The gap is 3/16" from the
factory, adjustable +3/32" / −1/32".

There are three types:
- **COMPACT CLIP:** a two-piece hinge with the plate pre-attached. The door clips on and off without tools.
  Cam adjustment in 3 directions and BLUMOTION built in.
- **COMPACT BLUMOTION:** one piece with soft-close in the cup. Height adjusts by loosening the screws.
- **COMPACT:** one piece with no soft-close. Soft-close is available as the 971A9700 add-on.

All three are interchangeable, but **don't mix them on one door**. BLUMOTION ships switched on, marked "I", and
can be switched off ("0") on one hinge of a light door.

## CLIP adjustments
- **Height:** cam on the mounting plate, ±2 to ±3mm depending on the plate.
- **Side (overlay):** front screw, ±2mm.
- **Depth (gap):** rear spiral cam, +3 / −2mm.
- **BLUMOTION on/off:** on CLIP top BLUMOTION, a switch on the cup turns soft-close off for one hinge of a
  light door. The door must be closed once for the change to take effect.

## TIP-ON for doors (touch to open)

- **Hinge type:** use **free swing hinges** with the magnet versions, and self-close or soft-close hinges with
  the bumper version. Works in frameless and blocked-out face frame cabinets.
- **956.1004:** standard doors up to about 52" tall. It has a magnet, about 17mm of push-out, and adjusts
  +4 / −1mm.
- **956A1004:** large doors (52" and up) and all inset doors. It has a magnet and about 38mm of push-out.
- **956A1006 / 956A1006F** (strong spring): large doors with a bumper, used with self-close or soft-close hinges.
- **Adapter plates:** 956.1201 in-line for standard doors, 956A1201 in-line for large doors, and 956A1501 wing
  for large doors. Colors are white, gray or black.
- Offset a screw-on catch plate 3mm from the TIP-ON unit. The self-adhesive catch plate won't stick to
  powder-coated, waxed or oiled surfaces.
