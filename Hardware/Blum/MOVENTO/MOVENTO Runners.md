# Blum MOVENTO Concealed Runners

Blum's premium concealed (undermount) runner line, one step up from TANDEM (see `../TANDEM/TANDEM Runners.md`). This
is where Blum's **heavy-duty** runners are.

Source: `MOVENTO Catalog (2026).pdf` (in this folder) (Blum, Inc., 2026). Everything below comes from that catalog.

## The family at a glance

| Series | Duty | Cabinet | Lengths | Load (static / dynamic) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **763H** | Standard | Face frame (with rear brackets) or frameless | 12", 15", 18", 21" | 125 / 110 lb |
| **763.** ("dot") | Standard | Frameless or blocked-out face frame only | 12", 15", 18", 21" | 125 / 110 lb |
| **769.** ("dot") | **Heavy** | Frameless or blocked-out face frame only | 18", 21", 24", 27", 30" | **170 / 155 lb** |
| **769R** | Heavy | Face frame or frameless (bottom mount) | 21" | 135 lb static |

Load ratings are per ANSI/BHMA A156.9 Grade 1, measured on a 21" drawer. All of them are full extension
with BLUMOTION soft-close. MOVENTO runners also have **built-in synchronization** that keeps the two sides
moving together, and they work with TIP-ON BLUMOTION and SERVO-DRIVE.

**There is no F (3/4" side) version of MOVENTO.** Every series takes 1/2" to 5/8" (13–16mm) drawer sides
only. If the drawer boxes have 3/4" sides, TANDEM 563F is the only option in these two catalogs.

The 769R is a pre-assembled bottom-mount **waste/recycle set** (769R5337SB / SD / SF for 12", 15" and 18"
minimum opening widths). It has 19mm (3/4") over-extension. It's a kit, not a general drawer runner, and
the lateral stabilizer can't be used with it.

## MOVENTO vs TANDEM

| | TANDEM 563H | MOVENTO 763H | MOVENTO 769. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Static / dynamic load | 100 / 90 lb | 125 / 110 lb | 170 / 155 lb |
| Lengths | 9"–21" | 12"–21" | 18"–30" |
| Side thickness | 1/2"–5/8" (563F for 3/4") | 1/2"–5/8" | 1/2"–5/8" |
| Inside drawer width | Opening minus 42mm | Same | Same |
| Max drawer height | Opening minus 21mm (13/16") | Opening minus **23mm (29/32")** | Same as 763H |
| Bottom clearance | 14mm (9/16") | **16mm (5/8")** | Same as 763H |
| Minimum rear notch | 35mm (1-3/8") | **50mm (1-15/16")** | Same as 763H |
| Locking devices | T51.1901 R/L | **T51.7601 R/L** | T51.7601 R/L |
| Synchronization | Optional lateral stabilizer | Built in | Built in |

The width math is the same as 563H. The outside-width deductions are also the same: 13/32" for 5/8" sides,
15/32" for 19/32", 5/8" for 1/2", and 23/32" for 15/32". **A drawer box built for TANDEM won't drop onto
MOVENTO.** It needs a 2mm shorter box, 2mm more bottom clearance, a deeper rear notch and different locking
devices.

**763H inside cabinet depth** (with rear attachment) is the same as TANDEM 563H. For example, 5330S takes a
minimum of 557mm (21-15/16") and a maximum of 599mm (23-19/32"), with a 548mm runner.

**763. and 769. minimum inside cabinet depth:** drawer length plus 20mm (13/16"). For example, a 21" runner
needs 553mm (21-13/16") and a 30" runner needs 782mm (30-13/16"). The runner itself is 9mm shorter than that.

## Other things to know

- **Thin sub-fronts:** the T59.K010M locking device spacer is for 12mm (15/32") and 13mm (1/2") sub-fronts.
  Without it, subtract 3mm (1/8") from the setback and screw locations.
- **Rear brackets:** the 763. and 769. series can't use rear brackets or sockets. The cabinet sides must be
  blocked out flush with the inside of the face frame.
- **Lateral stabilizer:** available for 763H, 763. and 769. (catalog p. 26). Not for 769R.
- **Bottom mount:** the catalog covers bottom-mount installs for 763H and 763. (p. 24) and a separate
  heavy-duty bottom-mount layout (p. 36).
- **TIP-ON BLUMOTION** comes in four strengths, sized by runner length and drawer weight. Minimum opening
  width is 281mm (11-1/16"). See catalog p. 18.
