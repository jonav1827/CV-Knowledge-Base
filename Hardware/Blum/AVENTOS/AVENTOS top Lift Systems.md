# Blum AVENTOS top Lift Systems

Blum's lift systems for wall and upper cabinet doors: HF top, HS top, HL top, HK top, HK-S and HK-XS. The
HKi, which is milled into the cabinet side, has its own file (`AVENTOS HKi Lift.md`).

Source: `AVENTOS top Catalog (2026).pdf` (in this folder) (Blum, Inc., 2026). Everything below comes from that
catalog. Many drilling positions are on drawings that came out garbled in the text copy. Those are marked
with a catalog page rather than guessed.

## Which lift for which door

| System | Door motion | Cabinet height | Min. inside depth | Hinges? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HF top** | Bi-fold: two doors fold up | 480–1219mm (18-7/8"–48") | 264mm (10-3/8") | Center hinge and door hinges, included in the hardware set |
| **HS top** | Up and over: one door swings up over the top | 350–800mm (13-3/4"–31-1/2") | 264mm (10-3/8") | No |
| **HL top** | Lift up: the door rises straight up | 300–580mm (11-13/16"–22-13/16") | 264mm (10-3/8") | No |
| **HK top** | Stay lift: the door pivots up | 205–610mm (8"–24") | 187mm (7-3/8"), or 261mm with a visible wall-hanging bracket | No |
| **HK-S** | Stay lift, compact | up to 610mm (24") | 163mm (6-1/2"), or 240mm with a visible wall-hanging bracket | No |
| **HK-XS** | Stay lift, smallest and cheapest | 238–610mm (9-3/8"–24") | 125mm (4-7/8"), or 100mm with a special drilling position | **Yes** (CLIP top or COMPACT) |

All of them go up to 1828mm (72") wide and are rated for 80,000 open/close cycles.

- **Low ceiling:** HK top barely rises above the cabinet. HF top folds, so it suits tall cabinets.
- **Crown molding over the cabinet:** HS top is the usual pick, but every system has a crown clearance
  formula (see each section below).
- **Appliance garage:** HL top with the 22L3900 lever set.
- **Very shallow cabinet:** HK-XS.
- **Thin doors (8mm and up):** HS, HL and HK top with the EXPANDO T door mounting plate 20S42T1.

## Sizing: power factor

**Power factor = cabinet height (in) × door weight (lb), with the handle weight counted twice.** HF top uses
the combined weight of both doors plus the handle.

- For overlapping ranges, Blum recommends the **stronger** mechanism, and a trial fit near the edges of a range.
- A **third lift mechanism** adds up to 50% power factor. Blum recommends one on wide cabinets so the door
  doesn't sag.
- HK-XS is sized per mechanism. **Double the power factor when using two.**
- HS top and HL top are sized by cabinet height and door weight instead (see their sections).

## Installing the lift mechanism (HF, HS, HL and HK top)

There are two fixing options, and each has its own part number:
- **Wood screws with the positioning system:** no pre-drilling. Four Ø4 × 35 wood screws per side.
- **Pre-attached system screws:** Ø5 holes drilled to the catalog pattern. These part numbers end in **10**
  (for example 22K2300 becomes 22K2310).

The mechanism's position in the cabinet and the door mounting plate position are fixed, so no layout math is
needed. The lift force is adjusted from the front, even with the cover cap on. Use a **POZI** driver, not a
Phillips, and a TORX T20 for adjustments.

## HS top: up and over

**Lift mechanisms:**

| Wood screws | System screws |
|---|---|
| 22S2200 | 22S2210 |
| 22S2500 | 22S2510 |
| 22S2800 | 22S2810 |

## HK-XS: smallest stay lift

It needs hinges. The lift handles the lifting and the hinges do the soft-close (with CLIP top BLUMOTION) or
free swing (with TIP-ON). **Designed for lift-up use only.**

**Lift mechanisms** (priced per mechanism, and the power factor doubles with two):

| Standard power factor | Standard | TIP-ON power factor | TIP-ON |
|---|---|---|---|
| 18–88 | 20K1101 | 16–70 | 20K1101T |
| 44–132 | 20K1301 | 44–106 | 20K1301T |
| 70–158 | 20K1501 | 70–141 | 20K1501T |

The opening angle is 105°, or less if the hinge opens less.

- **Cabinet mounting plate:** 20K5101 (frameless, screw-on), 20K51E1 (frameless, EXPANDO), 20K5501 (face frame).
- **Door mounting plate:** 20K4101 (screw-on), 20K41E1 (EXPANDO), 20K4101A (narrow aluminum), 20K4501 (large
  overlay 5-piece, which moves the hole location 19mm).
- **Hinge count:** use 3 hinges from a 900mm cabinet width or a power factor of 158, and 4 hinges at 1200mm
  width or a power factor of 238.
- **Hinges, soft-close (standard):**
  - CLIP top BLUMOTION 110°: 71B3590 (INSERTA) or 71B3550 (screw-on)
  - Narrow aluminum 95°: 71B950A
  - Face frame: COMPACT CLIP 31C358B.20 (1-1/4" overlay) or 30C258B.08 (1/2" overlay)
- **Hinges, free swing (TIP-ON):** CLIP top 110° 70T3590.TL (INSERTA) or 70T3550.TL (screw-on). Narrow
  aluminum 120°: 72T550A.TL.
- **Lift position:**
  - Y = 137 + D + K for frameless CLIP top, or 169 + D + K for face frame CLIP top.
  - Door plate at 126 + D + K + OL.
  - D = mounting plate height, OL = overlay, and K = hinge arm crank: 0 straight, 9.5 half-cranked, 18
    full-cranked. See p. 57.
- **Minimum inside height:** 200mm. That becomes 216mm with a 110° hinge on 20K5501, and 225mm with COMPACT
  on 20K5501.
- **Crown clearance:** X by door thickness is 16 → 45, 19 → 34, 22 → 23, 24 → 15.

## Shared parts and options

- **Cover caps (HF, HS and HL top):** 22.8000, or 23.8000 for SERVO-DRIVE (which includes the switches and Ø5
  distance bumpers). Colors are dark gray, silk white and light gray. Branding elements are optional:
  ABD.1000 / ABD.1009 series. Custom printing has a 1,000-piece minimum and embossing a 5,000-piece minimum.
- **Face frame mounting brackets** (for use without blocking out; 12.5mm (1/2") minimum recess, with M4 × 30
  screws):
  - HK top: 22K6001
  - HK-S: 20K6A01
  - HF top: 22F6001
  - HL top: 22L6001
  - *None is listed for HS top.*
- **Inset doors** (pp. 62–63) have two methods:
  - **Block out** the inside of the face frame so the blocking sticks into the opening, and treat its front
    as the cabinet face.
  - **Cabinet within a cabinet:** build a 4-sided liner box and mount as overlay on it.
  - HL top must mount **at least 10mm lower** than standard so the levers clear the top, and the door plate
    moves down by the same amount.
- **TIP-ON set:** 956.1004 (standard doors). Straight adapter plate: 956.1201. The catch plate sits 3mm from
  the TIP-ON when screwed on. Door gap is 2.6mm minimum with a glue-on catch plate and 3.1mm with a screw-on.
- **EXPANDO T** (8mm+ fronts, for HS, HL and HK top): door mounting plate 20S42T1. Minimum torque is 1.5 Nm
  in wood and MDF, 2 Nm in HDF, HPL and mineral composites, and 3 Nm in stone and ceramic.
- **SERVO-DRIVE** (electric touch-to-open):
  - Drive unit sets: **23.A000** for HF, HS and HL top, and **23KA000** for HK top. For three or more lift
    mechanisms, use two synchronized drive units.
  - Power: power supply set Z10NE04UG22, or the SERVO-DRIVE uno plug-in Z10NA40UG (one drive unit only).
    Power supply housing: Z10NG120.
  - Switches: extra switch 23P5020 (up to six per drive unit, and each switch pairs with one drive unit
    only). Switch adapter 23P5216 (no boring into the cabinet side).
  - The distance bumper is pressed in, not glued, and must not touch the switch. On HK top, don't put it in
    the upper third.
- **Assembly aids:**
  - Templates: universal drilling template ZML.0040.02, mounting plate template 65.5300, SERVO-DRIVE switch
    drilling template M31.2000, BLUMOTION/TIP-ON drilling template 65.5010.
  - PLATEMATE face frame adapter boring template: 65.5030.01.
- **Warranty:** limited lifetime to the original owner, and 5 years on Blum electrical components.
