# Blum AVENTOS HKi Lift System

A lift-up (stay lift) mechanism for wall and upper cabinet doors. For the rest of the AVENTOS family and
help choosing between them, see `AVENTOS top Lift Systems.md`. The door swings up and stays open. What
sets HKi apart from the other AVENTOS lifts is that the mechanism is **milled into the cabinet side panel**.
Fully integrated, the only visible part is the black lever. No hinges are needed.

Source: `AVENTOS HKi Catalog (2024).pdf` (in this folder) (Blum, Inc., 2024). Everything below comes from that
catalog. Many of the planning dimensions are on drawings that didn't come through cleanly in the text copy.
Those are marked "see catalog" rather than guessed.

## Specs

- Cabinet height: 162mm to 610mm (6-3/8" to 24")
- Cabinet width: up to 1828mm (72")
- Cabinet side panel: **16mm (5/8") minimum**
- Minimum inside cabinet depth: **271mm**
- Opening angle: 107°, with an adjustable opening angle stop built in
- BLUMOTION soft-close. A TIP-ON version is available for handleless doors.
- Three-way door adjustment with TORX screws. The lift force is adjusted from the front, ideally with a
  cordless driver.
- A built-in safety catch stops the lever from moving when no door is attached.

## Fully integrated vs semi-integrated

- **Fully integrated:** the mechanism is completely hidden inside the side panel. This takes a built-up side
  (facings over a milled raw board). Blum sells pre-milled MDF raw boards (24K7100, 2 × 600 × 450 × 19mm,
  moisture resistant) and a 4mm flush milling cutter (M45.ZF04) to expose the slot after the facings go on.
- **Semi-integrated:** the mechanism sits in a milled pocket on the inside face of the side, with a cover cap
  over it (24K8000.NA, in silk white, light gray or dark gray).

The milled slot dimensions for both are on catalog p. 8.

## Sizing: power factor

**Power factor = cabinet height (in) × door weight (lb)**, where the door weight **includes double the handle
weight**. Pick the lift mechanism whose range covers the power factor:

| Power factor | Lift mechanism set | TIP-ON version |
|---|---|---|
| 37–142 | 24K2300 | 24K2300T |
| 82–246 | 24K2500 | 24K2500T |
| 152–458 | 24K2700 | 24K2700T |
| 229–686 | 24K2800 | 24K2800T |

Each set is two symmetrical lift mechanisms in onyx black. A third mechanism can raise capacity by up to 50%,
and Blum recommends one on the center panel of wide cabinets so the door doesn't sag when open. Do a trial
fit when you're near the edge of a range or using a third mechanism.

*Example (my arithmetic, not from the catalog):* a 15" tall cabinet with a 10 lb door and a 1 lb pull is
15 × (10 + 2) = 180. That falls in both the 24K2500 and 24K2700 ranges, so it's a borderline case worth a
trial fit.

## Planning notes

- **Crown molding clearance** at full opening: **Y max = FH × 0.29 − 32 + FD**, where FH is the door height
  and FD is the door thickness (mm). Minimum reveal is 2mm. See catalog p. 10 for the Z table by door
  thickness.
- **Door bracket position** depends on the top reveal (Fo), side overlay (SFA) and side panel thickness (SWD).
  See catalog p. 9.
- **TIP-ON placement:** a 50mm minimum for standard doors and 76mm for doors over 400mm tall. Blum recommends
  offsetting a screw-on catch plate by 3mm. See catalog p. 11.
- **Installation:** slide the mechanism into the slot, turn the screw to press it into the cabinet, release
  the transport lock, clip on the door, then adjust.
- **Use a POZI driver, not a Phillips,** on Blum screws so they get full torque.
