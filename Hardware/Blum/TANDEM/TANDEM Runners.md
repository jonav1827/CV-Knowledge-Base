# Blum TANDEM Concealed Runners

Blum's concealed (undermount) drawer runners for wood drawer boxes. Shops call them "Tandem guides,"
"Blum guides," "undermounts" or "runners"; Blum's own catalogs say **runners**.

Source: `TANDEM plus BLUMOTION Catalog (2025).pdf` (in this folder) (Blum, Inc., 2025). Everything below
comes from that catalog unless it's marked otherwise.

## The family at a glance

| Series | Extension | Drawer side thickness | Lengths | Load (static / dynamic) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **563H** (TANDEM plus) | Full | 1/2" to 5/8" (13–16mm) | 9", 12", 15", 18", 21" | 100 / 90 lb |
| **563F** (TANDEM plus) | Full | 5/8" to 3/4" (16–19mm) | 9", 12", 15", 18", 21" | 100 / 90 lb |
| **563.** ("dot," TANDEM plus) | Full | 1/2" to 5/8" | 18", 21" only | 100 / 90 lb |
| **554H** (TANDEM edge) | 7/8 | 1/2" to 5/8" | 9", 12", 15", 18", 21" | 100 / 75 lb |

Load ratings are per ANSI/BHMA A156.9 Grade 1, measured on a 21" drawer. All of them have BLUMOTION
soft-close and are made in Blum's North Carolina plant.

## H vs F

**H is for 5/8" drawer sides and F is for 3/4" sides.** Everything else is the same: extension, load,
lengths, locking devices, rear brackets, mounting dimensions, screw locations, rear notch and hook bore.
The F runner sits farther out to clear the thicker side, so it takes a bigger width deduction:

| | 563H | 563F |
|---|---|---|
| Inside drawer width | Opening **minus 42mm (1-21/32")** | Opening **minus 49mm (1-15/16")** |
| Max drawer height | Opening minus 21mm (13/16") | Same |
| Bottom recess / bottom clearance | 13mm (1/2") / 14mm (9/16") | Same |

**Outside drawer width.** Measure the real side thickness and take this amount off the opening width:

563H:

| Side thickness | 5/8" (16) | 19/32" (15) | 9/16" (14) | 1/2" (13) | 15/32" (12) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deduct from opening | 13/32" (10) | 15/32" (12) | 9/16" (14) | 5/8" (16) | 23/32" (18) |

563F:

| Side thickness | 3/4" (19) | 23/32" (18) | 21/32" (17) | 5/8" (16) | 19/32" (15) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deduct from opening | 7/16" (11) | 1/2" (13) | 19/32" (15) | 21/32" (17) | 3/4" (19) |

Examples from the catalog: a 21" opening with 5/8" sides on 563H gives a 20-19/32" box. A 21" opening with
3/4" sides on 563F gives a 20-9/16" box.

## Parts shared by 563H and 563F

- **Locking devices:** T51.1901 R / T51.1901 L (standard, with side-to-side adjustment). One pair per drawer.
  Minimum inside drawer width is 170mm (6-11/16"). Other locking device options are on catalog p. 28.
- **Rear mounting bracket (face frame only):** 295.3750.03, non-handed, two per drawer. Use at least three
  screws per bracket. Frameless cabinets don't need it. The 9" runner has its own bracket options (catalog
  p. 31).
- **Screws:** 606N or 606P wood screws, or 662.1150.HG system screws.
- **Template:** T65.1600.01 Concealed Runner Template, for boring the locking device holes and rear hook holes.

## Drawer box prep (563H, 563F and 563.)

- **Rear notch:** 35mm (1-3/8") minimum, flush with the drawer sides and bottom. Or skip the notch by making
  the back shorter and running the bottom out to the back.
- **Hook bores:** 6mm diameter × 10mm deep, one in each end of the drawer back. The position dimensions (7mm,
  11mm and 13mm on the drawing) didn't come through clearly in the text copy, so check the diagram on catalog
  p. 11 or use the template.
- **Locking device holes:** 2.5mm pilot, 10mm deep, at the catalog's angle (use the template).

## The 563. ("dot") series

Made for 5/8" sides like the H, but only in 18" and 21". **Rear brackets and sockets can't be used with it**, so
the cabinet has to be frameless or a face frame blocked out flush with the inside of the frame. Its hole
pattern matches the 563H.

## Adjustments (563 series)

- Height: up to +3mm (1/8") at the locking device, tool-free.
- Side to side: ±1.5mm (1/16") at the front (locking device) and at the rear (runner), tool-free.
- Tilt: up to 5mm (3/16") rise of the drawer back, lever at the rear of the runner.
- Depth: ±4.5mm (3/16") using the elongated holes. The T51.1901.PS locking device adds up to 2.5mm (3/32").

## Wide drawers: lateral stabilizer

**ZST.686TU01** links the left and right runners with a rod so a wide drawer doesn't rack. It fits 563H, 563.
and 563F. Blum recommends it when the drawer is more than twice as wide as it is long. **It does not raise the
load rating.** Maximum opening width is 1352mm (53-7/32"). The runners sit 3mm (1/8") higher with it in
bottom mount installs.

## Heavy duty

**TANDEM is standard duty only.** There is no heavy-duty H or F. Blum's heavy-duty concealed runner is the
**MOVENTO 769.** (170 lb static, 18" to 30"). See `../MOVENTO/MOVENTO Runners.md`. MOVENTO only takes 5/8" sides, so
**a 3/4"-sided drawer can't go heavy duty in these catalogs.** 563F is the only 3/4" option.

*Unverified:* older Blum catalogs may have listed a TANDEM **569** rated heavier than the 563. If someone
asks for "heavy duty Tandems," they probably mean MOVENTO, or possibly that old 569.
